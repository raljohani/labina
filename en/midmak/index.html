About the work

An iPhone and iPad app for owners building their own home: every riyal recorded, every agreement kept, and your rights clear, from excavation to handing over the keys.

  • Expenses, contractors and daily labour in one logbook.
  • Site photos, invoices and documents in one place.
  • Log an expense by voice, with reminders for appointments and payments.
  • Your data stays on your device and in your own iCloud.
