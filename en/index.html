What these works share

Three different apps, one way of making them.

  • Your data stays on your deviceWhat you record is stored and processed on your device. We have no server to receive it.
  • No accounts, no adsOpen the app and start. We do not sell your attention to anyone.
  • Arabic from the first lineWords, numerals and direction are designed for Arabic, not translated into it.
