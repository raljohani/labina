About Labina

Labina is a small Saudi studio making Arabic apps for iPhone and Android. Its apps run on the user's device, with no accounts, ads or tracking. Its works today: Mizan, for managing spending from bank messages; Sana, on the Beautiful Names of Allah; and Midmak, for documenting the building of a home.

Logo

The logo is the word لبنة (Labina, "a brick") built in square Kufic: every cell is a brick, the dots glow in lantern amber, and the vowel marks are slanted bricks still being laid. The mark is the letter lām with the first brick.

  • Use the files as they are; do not set the name in another typeface instead.
  • Leave clear space of at least one brick around the logo.
  • Do not stretch, tilt, recolour or add shadows to the logo.
