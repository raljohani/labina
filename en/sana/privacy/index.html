Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Sana is an Arabic reference app about the 99 Names of God. It runs entirely on your device. We collect no data, require no account, and show no ads. The app makes no network requests; all content ships inside the app. No analytics, tracking, or third-party SDKs. Your progress, dhikr sessions, settings and reminders stay on your device (shared only with the app's own widgets, Live Activity and watch app on your devices). Exporting progress creates a file you choose where to keep. "Delete all my data" in Settings erases everything immediately. Notifications are requested only if you enable the daily reminder; saving a share card requests add-only Photos access, and the app never reads your photos.
