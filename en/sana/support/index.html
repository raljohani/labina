FAQ

The daily name reminder does not arrive

Turn the reminder on in Settings → Notifications, and make sure notifications for Sana are allowed in your device settings.

How do I move my progress to a new device?

Settings → Your data → "Export progress" saves a file you move yourself; then "Import progress" on the new device. No account or server keeps your data.

How do I show the tasbih in the Dynamic Island and Lock Screen?

In the Tasbih tab, tap the dotted-circle button in the top bar. Counting from the island needs iOS 17.2 or later.

How do I add Sana widgets?

Touch and hold the Home Screen or Lock Screen → Edit → Add Widget → "Sana".

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Your data → "Delete all my data". Everything is erased immediately and cannot be undone.
