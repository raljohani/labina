FAQ

Does Mizan read my messages?

On iPhone the app does not read your messages. You paste a bank message, or build your own Shortcuts automation that passes its text to Mizan. The message is processed on your device.

How do I move my data to a new device?

On iPhone, turn on iCloud sync in Settings and your data appears on your devices. Or create a backup in Settings, save the file, and open it on the new device.

How do I cancel Mizan Pro?

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Mizan → Cancel Subscription.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Backup → Delete all data. If iCloud sync is on, also remove the app's data from iCloud in your device settings.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com with your Apple Account.
